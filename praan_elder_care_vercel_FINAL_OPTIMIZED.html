The question you can't answer for them
What do your parents need to do today to stay fit and functional for the next few decades of their life?
The answer is almost always the same: build muscle. The programme starts there — 72 supervised strength sessions — and puts a doctor, a specialist and a nutritionist around it.

MUSCLE CURVE
Muscle & healthspan

Muscle mass and function aren't fixed.

Two things fall with age: muscle mass, and what the body can still do with it — function. That is the stairs they stop climbing, the bags they stop carrying, the chair they need help getting out of.

Progressive strength training is the only intervention shown to bend both curves upward at the same time. That is why 72 supervised sessions sit at the centre of this programme.

FAQ

Common questions

What exactly is my parent doing every week?
Three at-home 1:1 strength sessions with a Praan Coach and three live online group strength & conditioning sessions — six touchpoints a week, 72 supervised sessions across the three months. On top of that: a weekly nutrition consultation and a monthly doctor check-in.
Why is strength training the centre of the programme?
Muscle mass and function are the strongest predictors of independence in later life — they are what keep stairs, groceries and getting out of a chair easy. Progressive resistance training is the most direct way to rebuild both, so the doctors, specialists and diet plan are built around the training rather than the other way round.
Can I buy this for my parents?
Yes — that is who it is built for. You buy and pay; the care team then works directly with your parent. Plum stays your single point of contact for anything that comes up across the twelve weeks.
What happens before the training starts?
A comprehensive blood panel, an at-home physical assessment by a Praan physiotherapist, and a detailed health history intake with the care team. A Praan Doctor and the relevant specialist review all three and write the Personalised Care Protocol. The welcome kit with all the home equipment arrives before Day 1.
Which conditions is it built for?
The strength programme is delivered inside one of seven specialist protocols — diabetes, hypertension, arthritis and joint health, osteoporosis, Parkinson's, post-surgery recovery, or cardiac rehabilitation. Their Praan Doctor selects the protocol after reviewing diagnostics and history.
What if my parents live in another city?
The programme runs in Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Mumbai and the NCR. The assessment, the coach visits and the welcome kit are all arranged at their address, and the online sessions need only a phone or tablet. You can be looped in without being in the room.
My parent says they are fine and does not want fuss. Will this work for them?
It is built to feel like care, not a hospital. Sessions happen in their living room, the coach is the same person each time, and the protocol is written in plain language. Our advisors can also help you raise it with them.
Is the diet plan generic?
No. It is a therapeutic plan addressing their chronic conditions while supporting strength and recovery, built on the Praan Doctor's guidance and their existing eating habits — with a nutrition consultation every week.
Does this replace their doctor or medications?
No. Medication changes are made only by their treating doctor. The Praan Doctor coordinates the programme around their existing care and checks in monthly.
What does it cost?
The 3-month protocol is ₹54,999 in Bangalore, Hyderabad and Chennai, and ₹69,999 in Mumbai and the NCR — one payment, everything included. Further discounts apply on renewals and on adding family members.
